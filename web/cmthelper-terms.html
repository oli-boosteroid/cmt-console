cmthelper – Terms of Use

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Privacy Policy

What it is

cmthelper is a private moderation tool for r/BoosteroidCommunity. Only the moderators of that subreddit use it. It is not offered to other communities.

What it does

When a new post, comment or modmail message appears in the subreddit, the app sends a copy to a private Slack channel of the moderation team, so the team can follow up. Moderators can also send modmail replies through it.

The app does not post, vote, remove content or message anyone on its own. Every reply is written by a moderator.

Use

The app may change or stop working at any time. It is provided as is, without any guarantees.

Contact

Send a modmail to r/BoosteroidCommunity.
