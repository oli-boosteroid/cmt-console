cmthelper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Terms of Use

What the app reads

New posts, comments and modmail messages in r/BoosteroidCommunity: their text, the author's username, links and time. This is the same information the subreddit's moderators already see. Nothing from other subreddits.

Where it goes

To a private Slack channel of the moderation team, and from there to the team's internal support tool. Nowhere else.

Why

To answer questions and handle support requests from community members.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share this data, and do not use it for ads, profiling or tracking.

How long it is kept

Only as long as it is needed for support. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.

Contact

Send a modmail to r/BoosteroidCommunity.
